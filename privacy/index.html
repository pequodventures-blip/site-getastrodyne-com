Privacy

We do not sell or share contact information. When we write to you, we hold only the business contact details needed to correspond: name, work email address, company and role, and the history of our correspondence.

We use no tracking pixels and no click tracking in mail we send you. This website sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

To have your details removed, reply to any message from us with "no thanks", or write to ops@useastrodyne.com. We remove the record within five business days and add the address to a suppression list so it is not contacted again.

Questions about this policy: ops@useastrodyne.com.
